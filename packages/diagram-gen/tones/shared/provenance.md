# Provenance and redistribution decisions

The 24 bundled tone SVGs and their recipes are original exploratory studies already present in this MIT-licensed package. P01 preserves their bytes, stable IDs, editable sources, light/dark variants and original construction recipes. The shared explanation and per-tone reading guides are newly authored teaching text based on the visible drawings and their existing short explanation.

A historical source review motivated the composition topic. A private Composer manual was previously linked in each recipe and catalog entry. Those 48 current-tone URLs were removed because understanding the installed references must not require that repository, credentials or a website. This package does not redistribute that private manual, application screenshots, a proposal deck or private source snapshots. Newly authored explanations describe only the original conceptual drawings; they do not claim completeness or authorization to reproduce a private product specification.

Public inspiration links remain optional pointers to broad visual principles. They do not grant permission to copy third-party artwork, trademarks, text or design manuals, and none of that material is bundled here. The tone references are not copied brand assets. Consult the package LICENSE for this repository's original material; obtain separate rights for any third-party material supplied in a future brief.

The 48 replacement count covers `tones/catalog.json` and the 24 `tones/<id>/recipe.md` files only. Historical project example briefs and change records are separate provenance surfaces, not required resources of this tone collection. The tone importer now generates local descriptors and reading guides so regeneration cannot restore the removed required links.
