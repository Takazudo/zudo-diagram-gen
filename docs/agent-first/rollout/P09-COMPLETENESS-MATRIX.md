The kit inventory slot is 720 × 400 and repeats all available semantic symbols twice. Composition slots follow authored dimensions. These are actual declared evidence placements; smaller host slots need independent readability checks. Schemes retain six roles in both themes; a complete alternate palette is exercised without changing authored geometry.

Accepted pilot revisions fine/soft pilot-1, paper pilot-3 and pencil pilot-4 remain unchanged. All 72 original SVGs retain their protected hashes and ordered stable IDs. Native geometry is independently authored; hash difference is not an aesthetic character score. Actual P05 capture and per-image P03 rubric reviews assessed facts, warning/emphasis, clipping, labels and character. Thirteen textured/layered tones also received an independent second actual pass. Generator acceptance requires every current asset hash and full production placement hash to match its capture, every captured PNG hash to match a passing primary review, and each required independent review to match that same PNG. Changed inputs invalidate acceptance; exact unchanged SVG/PNG reuse is documented.

The strict bundled catalog gate rejects incomplete packs and unsupported references while custom legacy catalogs and ordinary legacy session/review loading stay compatible. Offline installed archive verification resolves all 24 contexts and repeatedly materializes both themes. Export uses saved candidate bytes; catalog upgrades do not rewrite reviewed artwork.

Initial factual/readability failures and evaluator disagreements are retained in [visual revision history](./P09-VISUAL-REVISION-HISTORY.json). Four frozen pilot pack compositions required corrected label association; isometric shadows required clearance; marker warning words required contrast. The corrected inputs passed actual recapture and evaluation, linked through exact SVG/PNG hashes in the final evidence artifact. These corrections do not alter accepted pilot pack resources or retrospectively rescore failed initial images. The generator recalculates acceptance rather than trusting the historical decision string.

Palette character limitations: chalkboard native light/dark palettes retain the dark board, while the alternate light surface changes that atmosphere despite recognizable chalk echoes and dust. Luminous-glass alternate light similarly weakens its native dark luminous mood. Reviewers accepted these remaining traits at character score 3 or higher; acceptance does not imply identical character across palettes.
