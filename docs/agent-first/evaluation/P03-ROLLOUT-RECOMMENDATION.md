These are version-specific prospective evaluations of unchanged or re-evaluated resources, not pooled interchangeable observations. Original full pilot:24 independent batches/120 initial outputs. Affected revision2:12/60; revision3:12/60; revision4:6/30. Across these experiments,270 initial outputs and120 correction copies were retained,390 captures total. Every initial/changed corrected PNG was actually opened; unchanged correction copies were verified against prior actual views. The original120-output requirement is complete and remains visible, including failures. Empty aborted dispatches and environment interruptions are separately retained outside completed counts.

Fine outline and filled forms transferred with their own construction. Paper needed substantive filled/layered planes rather than pale removable strips. Pencil needed real neighboring crossings and a clear connection between texture and principal object/backing/route construction. The final guidance preserves factual vacancy, upright Japanese labels, flexible geometry and the common semantic palette. No mandatory motif, coverage quota, distorted glyphs or lowered score threshold was introduced. Each tone’s seven primitives and documented alternatives remain distinct; existing examples are not replaced by pilot artwork.

Schemes and kits do not prevent label/connector collisions or prove factual correctness. Required actual placement inspection and bounded corrections remain part of authoring. Resource bytes increased and output sizes varied; token counts, deployed model snapshot, generation seed and enforceable token ceilings were unavailable. Two repetitions do not establish statistical significance, latency savings, fewer corrections or general quality guarantees. Manager review was unblinded; independent paper/pencil reviews used opaque run IDs and no condition resources. Historical evaluator disagreements were retained, not averaged away.

Unresolved character failures in earlier revisions remain documented in [revision1](./P03-REVISION1-REPORT.md), [revision2](./P03-REVISION2-REPORT.md) and [revision3](./P03-REVISION3-REPORT.md). The accepted paper and pencil resources have complete new affected pairs under the unchanged rubric. Local Chromium151 and the recorded Noto font were held constant; pinned CI browser checks are separate. Large PNGs stay local/ignored with hashes and rerenderable SVG/provenance evidence committed. Dark preparation is distinct from the light paired trial; production palette remapping and every later tone still need light/dark placement review.

P03 implementation/package checks and current-base integration must pass before its PR merges. A passing pilot then permits P04 production materialization and, after that dependency merges, P09 all24-tone work. Neither this recommendation nor a passing schema completes issue66: supported CLI/skill, full catalog, onboarding documentation, and the integrated five-session trial remain separately gated.
