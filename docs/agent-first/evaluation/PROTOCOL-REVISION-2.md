## Why a new revision

Revision 1 produced 120 initial light outputs across 24 independent batches. The coordinator inspected every initial capture and the bounded correction captures; independent second reviews covered all 12 paper/pencil batches. The original gate fails on paper/pencil character even after factual/readability corrections. Some independent pencil reviews scored retained notebook geometry more favorably than others; those judgments remain visible, rather than being rewritten as unanimous failure or overridden to pass.

Visible paper construction repeatedly reduced broad overlapping cut planes and patterned surfaces to separate plain tilted cards with shadows or top rules. Pencil retained ruling and small diagonal marks but often lost prominent crossed hatch fields and visible retracing. Paper A also weakens, so the evidence does not isolate loss to adding the scheme or kit. The revision is an attempt to improve construction transfer, not a proven repair or a general efficiency claim.

The original example teaches Pattern + Text → composition → PNG. Its exact labels, central poster merge, arch motif and file-output icon are example content. The revised rules transfer cut-plane construction and crossed pencil marks without importing those facts into another brief. Essential Japanese labels still use the common declared Noto Sans CJK JP/sans-serif fonts; palette remains the same six roles in both themes. Original Latin typography and warm paper colors do not override those controls.
