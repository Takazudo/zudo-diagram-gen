---
title: New sessions use zfb 3
---

New diagram sessions use zfb and zfb-runtime 3.2.0 with zudo-react page generation. The engine selects the page renderer from the session’s installed zfb and continues supporting existing zfb 2.21.1 hosts.

The package-owned review interface, SVG files, feedback, and offline exports keep their existing contracts. The documentation showcase remains on zfb 2 and zudo-doc 5; its migration is deferred. No packages were published as part of this change.
