Released: 2026-09-27

### Added

- A local SVG review workbench for comparing candidates, recording feedback, and refining a selected drawing while preserving earlier versions.
- Twenty-four tone references and six complete worked example sessions.
- A destination-based initializer and commands for checking sessions, exporting SVGs, and creating an offline review page.

### Changed

- The project site now lives at [zudo-diagram-gen-doc.zudolab.dev](https://zudo-diagram-gen-doc.zudolab.dev/) and uses the zudo-doc documentation home.
- The tone collection moved from `/tones/` to `/docs/tones/`; examples moved from `/examples/` and `/examples/<slug>/` to `/docs/examples/` and `/docs/examples/<slug>/`; the workbench moved from `/workbench/` to `/docs/workbench/`. Existing `/docs/` pages keep their URLs.
- Browser-stored reviews from the old site do not carry over to the new hostname. Before switching, download your review JSON from the old site; then open the matching session on the new site and import that file.
