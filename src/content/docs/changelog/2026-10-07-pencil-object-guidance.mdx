---
title: Pencil object construction guidance
---

The pencil pilot scheme now clarifies that principal explanatory objects and backing carry the sketch construction; a detached header or footer hatch band alone is insufficient. Readable label/detail emphasis and visible retracing remain important, with upright Japanese text and flexible routing.

Existing kit geometry and texture algorithms remain unchanged. The prospective revision-four protocol requires six fresh pencil batches under the existing rubric; preparation and deterministic checks do not establish acceptance.
