---
title: Four pilot tone packs with native semantic kits
---

Fine outline, soft fill, paper layers and pencil notebook now include validated schemes and seven tone-native semantic symbols: person, pin, slot card, empty seat, arrow, check and clock. Open contours, rounded filled forms, overlapping cut pieces and retraced pencil geometry remain separate construction methods. The original 72 source/light/dark SVGs and all tone IDs are preserved.

Schemes define geometry, typography, semantic palette, texture and composition rules with required, preferred and flexible obligations. Recipe numbers resolve from scheme references. A repository authoring tool reproduces committed kits from separate SVG templates and a fixed pencil texture seed; importer regeneration preserves those templates, numeric bindings and resource declarations.

These four packs are prepared inputs for the frozen repeated paired evaluation. Deterministic validation and preliminary kit inspection do not establish the 120-output visual gate, Japanese readability, fewer corrections or a supported all-tone rollout. Those conclusions remain open until the recorded experiment passes.
