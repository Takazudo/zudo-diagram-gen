title: ZDG homepage logo
---

The homepage now displays the owner's updated ZDG logo, retaining the bird, fish, and rectangular frame. The supplied SVG is used unchanged on its 1200 × 630 canvas.

The existing zudo-doc logo setting loads the asset as a theme-adaptive CSS mask. The supplied lettering remains editable SVG text using `Futura-Medium, Futura`; no font is embedded, so its appearance depends on the fonts available in the viewing environment. The favicon, navigation, and workbench are unchanged.
