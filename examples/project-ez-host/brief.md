# zudo-ez-host — Saving and publishing

Three distinct domains: local folder, saved cloud checkpoint, and live site. Separate Save and Publish arrows retain the explicit publication boundary.

## Shared facts

- Local folder, saved cloud checkpoint, and live site are separate places.
- Save and Publish are separate actions. Saving alone does not mean the live site changed.
- The combined Push & publish action, failure states, history restore, and merge are outside this focused diagram.

## Scope and limits

Conceptual actions are labeled Save and Publish for legibility. Actual product labels include Push/Save and Publish saved. The combined Push & publish shortcut and failure states are omitted from this focused study.
